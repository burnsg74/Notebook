Role: Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
Dates: May 2001 – March 2003

Started working for AsiaTel, a calling card company startup, as CTO. As one of the company's initial employees, was tasked with building the technical foundation of the business from the ground up.

Managed all IT responsibilities, including installing and maintaining Linux servers, Cisco routers, Ethernet switches, hubs, and Lucent EXS-2000 telecom equipment. This required a broad skill set spanning networking, systems administration, and telecom hardware.

Beyond infrastructure, also designed, developed, and deployed the company's website and CRM system, gaining early exposure to full-stack development work that would define much of the later career.
