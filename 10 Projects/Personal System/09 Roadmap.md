> What should be true in 3, 6, and 12 months for each goal. Review monthly. AI: use this to check whether `08 Next Steps` is pointed the right way, and flag milestones that are slipping.

## Direction in one line

Get back to a stable remote engineering income first; let health, systems, and apps support that, not compete with it.

_These milestones are a first draft based on `02 Goals` and [[4 - Action Plan]]. Change any that feel wrong or too big._

## Milestone format

```
- [ ] What will be true | goal: work | by: 2027-01-24
```

`goal` uses the same values as `08 Next Steps`. Check a milestone when it's true, and log it in `10 Log/Progress`.

- [ ] At least three close-fit interviews | goal: work | by: 2027-01-24

## Monthly review

About 20 minutes, once a month:

1. Check off milestones that are true.
2. For each slipping milestone: is it still right, too big, or blocked? Shrink it, move its date, or drop it.
3. Make sure `08 Next Steps` has at least one task feeding each open 3-month milestone.
4. Update the `updated` date.

## If things go sideways

- **Bad health stretch:** pause app and tool work first. Keep only the job search minimum and the health log.
- **Money emergency:** budget and income tasks come before everything else until it's handled.
- **Job offer arrives early:** skip ahead to the 6-month section and re-plan.
