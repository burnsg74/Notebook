## Summary

25+ years in software, nearly all remote and at small companies. Started in Army satellite communications, moved into telecom and VoIP, then CTO and founder roles, then senior full-stack engineering from 2016 on. Last engineering role ended October 2024 when a government contract ended. Since then: a 12-month search, a Class A CDL (July 2025), and a full-time job as a truck driver for a local lumber store (from November 2025) to cover bills.

## Positioning

- **Identity:** Senior / principal full-stack engineer for small remote product teams.
- **Lead with:** React + TypeScript frontend, AWS serverless backend (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB), Python. Proof: Makpar.
- **Supporting depth:** 10+ years of PHP/Laravel and eCommerce backends (Magento, Commerce7).
- **Leadership:** Former CTO (twice) and founder; led teams at Red Pocket, Calltext, and Makpar.
- **Don't:** open with a long list of languages, or lead with truck driving in technical applications.
- **Can't do (skip these jobs):** Ruby on Rails, React Native, BigCommerce, Salesforce, Django, Java, Spring Boot, UI/UX design, Google Apps, logistics and supply chain, Shopify. From [[10 Clients/Work/Alfred/Job Hunt/5-Notes/Skill gaps]].

## Stories to tell in interviews

Prepare these as short stories (problem, what I did, result), not memorized answers.

1. **Makpar SBA portal:** leading a team on a government system that had to be secure, tested, and correct.
2. **Ronati inventory sync:** validation and idempotent writes under deadline; Python scrapers on Lambda.
3. **GSATi:** shipping a new Commerce7 plugin while keeping a legacy Laminas codebase alive.
4. **Calltext CRM:** Twilio and SendGrid behind one surface, with rate limits to stay inside provider quotas.
5. **Red Pocket CTO:** growing from engineer to CTO, CRM migration to AWS, hiring and managing.

## Interview strengths and weak spots

- **Strong:** architecture talk, past decisions, "how would you approach this", explaining to non-technical people.
- **Weak:** live timed coding, whiteboards, rapid-fire questions. I work best with time to think.
- **Rule:** ask about the interview format before committing time; prefer founder or hiring-manager conversations.

## Why the last search stalled (Oct 2024 – Oct 2025)

Not a performance issue: the SBA contract ended and Makpar had no project to move me to. The search hit a bad market (layoffs, AI uncertainty, 500–2,000 applicants per role), keyword-matching applicant tracking systems that read broad experience as unfocused, automated coding screens, and my weak live-interview performance. Unemployment ran out in September 2025. Full analysis: [[10 Clients/Work/Alfred/Job Hunt/5-Notes/1-Job Hunting/3 - What went wrong - Post October 2024]].
