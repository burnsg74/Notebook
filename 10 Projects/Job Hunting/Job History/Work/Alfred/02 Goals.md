> My primary goals, why each matters, and what "good enough" looks like. AI: use this to judge whether a suggestion moves me toward a goal or away from one.

## The one-line version

A happy, stable life: steady income from a remote job that will last, better health, and systems that handle the everyday so I don't have to figure it out each time.



## Life goals

These are the outcomes. Everything else serves them.

### 1. Happy, stable life
- **Why:** Life has demanded a lot from me. I spent years raising children, with my time belonging to them and the family. On top of that, I changed jobs every few years, and each move meant learning new systems from scratch more time pulled away from me. I'm in my 50s now. My children are mostly grown, and I want a stable job I don't have to replace every few years — one I can perfect, with systems in place that run without constant attention. For the first time, I want room for myself: time to find and do the things I enjoy, instead of only what everyone else needs from me. 

- **Good enough looks like:** A good job that will last more then a few years. 

### 2. Earn enough to pay the bills
- **Why:** I do not need to be rich, just effort to pay bills and live off of where I do not need to stress about money.
- **Good enough looks like:** at least $3,000/month take-home.

### 3. Health: fewer headaches, less depression
- **Why:** Headaches and depression drain the energy every other goal needs.
- **Good enough looks like:** Able to manage health issues with out adding stress about getting the work I need to get done done. That might mean having the flexible to take breaks during the day when needed. 

### 4. Work: stable remote job that will last

- **Why:** After not being able to find work after Makpar I had to pause my career as a Full Stack Engineer. To get back to it these are the steps I think it is going to take.  
- **Good enough looks like:** Work from home, steady employer or client base, skills that stay in demand as AI changes the field.
  - **Role:** remote full-stack, principal, or tech lead
  - **Company:** small, about 10–50 people (up to ~200 if remote-first), founder or hiring manager visible
  - **Pay:** $110k–$140k (equity okay), or contract at $30/hr or more
  - **Interviews:** based on past work and approach, not whiteboard speed
  - **First step:** any good-enough remote programming job; find the ideal one after that, from a position of employment.
- **Details:** Thru Stages: 
1. Stable Temp job to pay bills
2. Get back to home office with remote job
3. Build Freelance Clients and Profiles on Sites like Upwork, and Fivver.
4. Build clients in my own website Burns Web Services doing a niche I can get good at.
