## 1. The core problem, named plainly

You don't have a tools problem. You have a **single-source-of-truth problem** plus a **capacity problem** (headaches, full-time job, family time, low energy after work). Every tool you listed is fine in isolation — Cursor, Wispr Flow, Warp, NotebookLM — the failure mode is that _plans live in places you don't revisit_. So the fix isn't a new app, it's:

- One place plans live (Obsidian)
- One recurring moment you actually open it (a ritual, not willpower)
- A structure small enough to maintain in 10–15 minutes, tired, with a headache

## 2. Obsidian structure (minimal, not a system to maintain)

`0-Dashboard.md        <- the ONLY file you open daily 1-Job Hunting/        (you already have this) 2-Freelance-BWS/ 3-Learning/ 4-Home-Life/ 5-Notes/`

**0-Dashboard.md** is a single note with a Dataview or Tasks query pulling open tasks tagged `#next`, plus 3 manually-updated sections:

- **This Week's One Priority** (just one — job app push, or learning, or Michael follow-up)
- **Waiting On** (Michael, applications pending response, interview scheduled)
- **Energy Log** (headache day? low/med/high — 1 tap, helps you see patterns over weeks)

Everything else — job leads, learning notes, budget thoughts — gets its own file, but tasks get tagged so they surface on the dashboard. You never "go find" the plan; the plan finds you when you open one note.

## 3. Weekly rhythm around your actual time slots

Given: full-time driving job, home only nights/weekends, low energy after work, headaches unpredictable, wife-adjacent laptop time, alone-focus time, tablet-in-bed time, 10-min commute audio.

|Slot|Best use|
|---|---|
|10-min commute (audio)|Passive: podcast on interviewing, AI tools, or **your own recorded notes played back** (record yourself talking through a plan, replay it)|
|Tablet in bed|Read-only / low-effort: review job leads, read learning material, journal in Obsidian mobile — never coding, never job applications (too high-friction on a tablet)|
|Laptop w/ wife nearby|Low-focus tasks: applications, resume tailoring, budget entry, email to Michael — social-compatible tasks|
|Alone + focused|High-focus: coding practice, Cursor/AI learning, interview prep, freelance client work|
|Weekday mornings (5–7am, per your own boundary)|Cross-timezone overlap only — don't overload this with job hunting too|

This assigns _task type to context_, not time-block scheduling you'll fail to hit. On a bad headache day, you just do tablet-tier tasks and don't feel behind — the system degrades gracefully.

## 4. Prioritization: what actually competes for your evening/weekend hours

Right now you're splitting energy across: job applications, Michael/network follow-up, freelance/BWS, learning Cursor/AI, budget app, interview prep, and CDL-job stability. That's too many fronts for the actual hours available (roughly 15–20 hrs/week after work + chores).

I'd suggest a **weekly split**, revisited every Sunday in 15 min:

- **60% job search** (applications + Michael follow-up + interview prep) — this is the fastest path to income/remote flexibility
- **25% learning** (Cursor fluency, AI-model literacy — this directly serves job search, so it's not separate, it's fuel for it)
- **15% freelance/BWS** — kept alive but not primary, since cold outreach with a mailto CTA and no portfolio traction is a long game. Don't let this compete with job search hours until job search shows real signal.

**Budget app**: park it. Use a boring spreadsheet or free tool (even Apple's built-in) for now — cap it at maybe 2 hours total to get "will I go negative" visibility, then stop. It's a good learning project _later_, not survival infrastructure now.

## 5. Michael / the maybe-job lead

This needs a lightweight, low-effort touch cadence so it doesn't die from neglect but doesn't feel needy either. Suggested: one note in `2-Freelance-BWS/` or a `Network` folder logging last contact date + next planned touch, and a recurring **monthly**(not weekly) low-key check-in — share a small win, an article, or progress on the ERPNext thing, not "any update?" messages.

## 6. Interviews

Given anxiety + limited windows: batch interview _prep_ into your alone-focus weekend time, and request interviews be scheduled **evenings or Saturdays** upfront when companies propose times — most small startups you're targeting (Wellfound/Otta-type) are flexible about this. A recording+feedback tool is a reasonable add later, but start cheap: record yourself with your phone/Loom answering common questions, review once — that alone cuts anxiety a lot for near-zero setup cost.
